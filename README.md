# F&B 2027 Margin Stress Test

Review copy of Piquant Ideas' free scorecard for Singapore F&B operators. It estimates what the 2027 food-cost wave (El Niño) and the RTS Link opening (February 2027) could do to an operator's monthly profit, gives a resilience score out of 100, and suggests three moves to make now.

**Live review link:** https://piquantideas.github.io/biz-stress-test/

## For reviewers

1. Open the link and run through it as an F&B operator you know well.
2. Try the Moderate and Severe scenarios on the results page.
3. Send feedback to Celestine (celestine@piquantideas.com) on:
   - Questions that are unclear, or answer ranges that don't fit real operators
   - Whether the stress-test numbers feel believable
   - Whether the three moves feel specific and urgent enough
   - Anything missing for your type of F&B business

This copy doesn't save anything you enter.

## How it works

- Money questions use ranges; the calculation uses each range's midpoint.
- Moderate scenario: exposed ingredients cost 20% more through 2027. Severe: 35%.
- RTS impact is estimated from outlet location and how easily customers could find similar food cheaper in Johor Bahru.
- Score = stress test (up to 50) + readiness (up to 50). Below 40: act now. 40–64: exposed. 65+: prepared.

All figures are illustrative estimates, not financial forecasts.

**Link:** https://piquantideas.github.io/biz-stress-test/scorecard/

One free scorecard for Food & Beverage, Professional services, Beauty, health or wellness, E-commerce and Others, running on scoring library v1.1 (see the PRI Scoring Workbook). Add a tag after `#` to record where a respondent came from, for example `.../scorecard/#bni.pilot`.
