How the F&B stress test works

Each money answer is a range, and the calculation uses its midpoint. These are illustrative estimates to start a conversation, not forecasts. Adjust the assumptions here once you have real client data.

The stress test

Profit today = monthly sales × net margin Extra food cost = sales × food cost % × exposed share × shock (20% moderate, 35% severe) Absorbed = extra food cost × (1 − share passable to customers) Sales lost to JB = sales × location factor × how easily customers find it cheaper in JB Profit lost (RTS) = sales lost × (1 − food cost %) rent and staff stay the same Profit in 2027 = profit today − absorbed − profit lost (RTS)

The score

Stress points (0–50) = margin in 2027 mapped from −5% → 0 to 12% → 50 (moderate scenario) Readiness points (0–50) = readiness answers (each 0–3, seven questions) ÷ 21 × 50 Score = stress points + readiness points Bands = Act now below 40 · Exposed 40–64 · Prepared 65+

Which three moves are shown

Urgent moves come first: Rebuild the concept if the 2027 margin falls under 3%, then a move for each readiness answer scoring 0 or 1, then Make the RTS work for you if 2% or more of sales could cross the border. Remaining slots are filled with opportunities: the Mixue supply model, OEM production, packaged products, then supplying other kitchens.
