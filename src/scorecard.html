How this score is calculated

The Piquant Relevance Index runs from 0 to 100, and higher is safer. Half is Readiness: how prepared each of the 8 parts of your business is, weighted for your industry. Half is the Profit buffer: how much margin is left after the risks ahead, each weighed by how likely and how soon it is.

Below 40 means act now, 40 to 64 means exposed, 65 and above means prepared. Because the score comes from your own answers, it is shown as a range of about 13 points either side. A full audit narrows that range with evidence.
